A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Questions?
We’ve got you.

A little clarity before their first visit. For anything specific to your pup, ask the salon directly.

What’s the difference between Mini Groom and Full Groom?+

The salon’s comparison lists a face, ears, feet and tail tidy in Mini Groom. Full Groom also includes the body trim.

How much will my dog’s groom cost?+

Ask the salon for a current quote for your dog and chosen service. Confirm the price before booking.

Are dye and flea/tick shampoo part of the package?+

The published comparison marks both as extra-charge options across all four packages. Ask the team about availability and suitability.

How do I request an appointment?+

Use WhatsApp or the enquiry builder on the contact page. Your message starts a conversation; the salon confirms the appointment.

Where is the salon?+

At 1802 W Hildebrand Ave, San Antonio, TX 78201. Open the directions link to plan your visit.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?+

Contact the team as soon as possible. Check the salon’s current cancellation and no-show policy on Instagram before booking.
